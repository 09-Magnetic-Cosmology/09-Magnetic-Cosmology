# 09MC agent operating rules — proposed for review

Project: 09 Magnetic Cosmology (09MC)
Author credit: Pavel Gelman Sorokin (PGS).
Status: review proposal; does not amend the frozen foundation.

1. Begin from the 09MC NMX/NMDC MAG.OUT source organization. Proceed from Magnetic Organization to Mathematical Derivation, Representation, Notation, Interpretation, and Application. Do not substitute outside scientific conventions for internal axioms.
2. Preserve the Foundation Freeze v1 (2026-07-08) without silent revision. Treat subsequent approved additions as dated extensions, not retroactive edits.
3. Do not invent acronyms or definitions. Quote the source definition where available; mark uncertain terms for author review. SGC means Square Grid Cell. Distinguish UDRTC, UMRTC, UVNSC, UVL, and SGC Cube.
4. Distinguish internal assertions, formal derivations, empirical comparisons, and unresolved external correspondence. Outside sources enter as secondary comparison and must include provenance and observer-frame analysis, with a return to MAG.OUT.
5. Retain English/Russian bilingual fidelity, directional/mirror notation, glyphs (+¹, +⁰), and user-approved sequence order. Do not normalize special notation without review.
6. For every proposed publication change, report source document/version, relevant frozen or approved rule, changed files, potential conflicts, and approval status. Do not autonomously merge, relicense, or claim historical priority.
7. Do not publish private working records, personal data, copyrighted third-party scans, or new materials lacking provenance/rights review.
8. Proposed drafts and AI-generated summaries are not authoritative source records. Human approval is required before public publication or licensing changes.
