## Proposed future scope
1. **Original software/code only:** consider Apache License, Version 2.0, with an exact canonical license file, copyright notice, and any necessary NOTICE file. Do not apply before authorship, contributions and third-party dependencies are checked.
2. **Original scientific monographs and writing:** select separately between retaining All Rights Reserved, CC BY 4.0 (adaptations allowed with credit), or CC BY-ND 4.0 (redistribution without adapted publication). Confirm appropriate handling of text and images and whether any source material has been distributed under earlier terms.
3. **Original figures, drawings and 3D models:** evaluate work-by-work; files may need different licenses from code used to generate them.
4. **Historical scans, quotations and other third-party source material:** no new license is granted; include source-specific attribution and rights information.
5. **Trademarks, authorship and scientific priority:** open-source and CC licenses do not independently establish priority over underlying ideas, nor do they guarantee preservation of an author's scientific interpretation.

## Before any license is made effective
- Inventory every published path and its rights holder, original publication date, and previous permissions.
- Resolve which works are wholly original, jointly authored, AI-assisted, or third-party.
- Decide adaptation and commercial reuse permissions for each content class.
- Obtain legal review if patents, third-party contributions, or prior grants of rights are relevant.
- Add precise per-folder or per-file scope and explicit effective-date language.
- Keep a record of review and author approval before merging.
